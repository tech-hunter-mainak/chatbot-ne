Here is a concise summary of the Nagaland State Portal's "People & Culture" section:

Nagaland is home to 17 major tribes, distinct languages, and various celebrations that light up the calendar. The state has a rich culture, with a strong focus on hospitality and song.

**Tribes:**

1. Angami
2. Ao
3. Chakhesang
4. Chang
5. Konyak
6. Khiamniungan
7. Kuki
8. Kachari
9. Lotha
10. Phom
11. Pochury
12. Rengma
13. Sumi
14. Sangtam
15. Tikhir
16. Yimkhiung
17. Zeliang

**Festivals:**

1. Hornbill Festival (seasonal)
2. Sekrenyi (February 25 - Angami tribe)
3. Moatsu (May 2 - Ao tribe)
4. Sökrönye (January 15 - Chakhesang tribe)
5. Naknyulüm (July 31 - Chang tribe)
6. Aoleang (April 1-2 - Konyak tribe)
7. Tuluni (July 8 - Sumi tribe)
8. Tokhu Emong (November 6-7 - Lotha tribe)
9. Yemshe (October 5 - Pochury tribe)

**Culture:**

* Traditional attire features headdress craft, bamboo weave, and hornbill feathers.
* Textile motifs feature geometric patterns that map clan, valley, and story.
* Evolving fashion blends heritage cloth with contemporary cuts.
* Cuisine features bamboo shoot, axone (fermented soybean), smoked meats, and foraged greens.

**Community:**

* Village homestays offer opportunities to learn cooking and crafts.
