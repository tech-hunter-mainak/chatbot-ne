Here is a concise summary of the Sanjeevani Village Health Outreach Programme:

**Overview**

* Sanjeevani is a once-a-month, fixed-date outreach initiative that provides converged health services at the village level.
* The program aims to early identify, screen, refer, and follow up patients with chronic diseases.

**Key Features**

* 80 Mobile Health Units (MHU) are being introduced throughout the state.
* Each MHU is manned by a team of healthcare professionals.
* Services aim to provide free medicines for effective chronic disease management.
* The program covers about 60 lakh people in rural Assam every month.

**Objectives**

* Augment, complement, and supplement existing public health infrastructure in rural Assam.
* Monitor and manage beneficiary's disease management on a monthly basis.
