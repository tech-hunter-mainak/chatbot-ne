Here is a concise summary of the PPP with Charitable Hospital scheme:

**Scheme Overview**

* The scheme aims to provide healthcare services to urban poor populations in Assam.
* NHM, Assam has signed MoUs with private hospitals and charitable trusts to improve maternal and child health.

**Services Provided by Charitable Hospitals**

* Maternal and child health services
* Vaccination of children
* Routine Ante-Natal care
* Basic laboratory services
* Delivery of pregnant mothers in hospital
* Family Planning services
* OPD services
* Awareness on population stabilization

**Financial Support**

* An amount of Rs. 15,00,000/- is provided to the Hospital Management Committee per annum.
* The fund will be utilized for monthly outreach/urban camps, medicines, manpower, and maintenance of the hospital.

**Guidelines**

* The allotted fund will be released in two instalments.
* Monthly reporting of services is mandatory.
* Hospitals are required to provide documents related to hospital records as and when required.
