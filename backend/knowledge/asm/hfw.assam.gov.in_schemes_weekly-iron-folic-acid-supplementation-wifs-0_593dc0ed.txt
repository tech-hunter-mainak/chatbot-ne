Weekly Iron Folic Acid Supplementation (WIFS) Programme:

* Objective: To control anaemia in adolescent boys and girls aged 10-19 years.
* Goal: To implement a school and anganwari-based weekly IFA supplementation programme for controlling anaemia.
* Strategy:
	+ Weekly administration of 100mg elemental iron and 500ug Folic acid supplements (IFA tablet) for 52 weeks.
	+ Screening for moderate/severe anaemia and referral to health facilities.
	+ Biannual deworming with Albendazole 400mg.
	+ Dissemination of information on nutrition and prevention of intestinal worm infestation.

Location: Implemented in urban and rural areas through Government/Government aided/municipal schools and Anganwari centres.
