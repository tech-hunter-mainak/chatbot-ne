**Scheme Overview**

* PPP with Tea Garden Hospitals: A public-private partnership scheme launched in 2007-08 to provide healthcare services to tea garden workers and their families.
* Launched by National Health Mission (NHM), Assam, to address health disparities among marginalized communities.

**Key Features**

* 150 tea garden hospitals are currently covered under the scheme.
* Funds allocated: Rs. 7.5 lakh per hospital as central assistance under PPP.
* Scheme aims to provide maternal and child healthcare, emergency services, referral services, communicable and non-communicable disease care, and infrastructure development.

**Guidelines**

* Agreement signed between District Health Society and Tea Garden Hospital Management Society.
* Hospitals must adhere to schematic guidelines and norms set by NHM, Assam.
* Funds released in two installments: 1st after MoU signing, 2nd after submitting UC and SoE of 1st installment.

**Implementation**

* Presently, 150 tea garden hospitals are covered under the scheme.
* Aim is to cover more tea garden hospitals in the near future.
