**Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)**

* Launched by Government of India on June 1, 2011
* Entitles pregnant women delivering in public health institutions to free and no expense delivery, including C-section
* Includes free drugs, diagnostics, blood, diet, transportation, and treatment for newborns up to 1 year old

**Entitlements under JSSK**

* Free drugs and consumables
* Free diagnostics
* Free C-section
* Free treatment and delivery
* Free blood transfusion
* Free transportation
* Free diet

**Other Schemes and Services**

* Assam Arogya Nidhi (AAN)
* Chief Minister’s Free Diagnostic Services
* Community Action for Health
* Comprehensive Abortion Care
* Free Drugs Service
* Free Operations for Children Having Congenital Heart Disease
* Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)
