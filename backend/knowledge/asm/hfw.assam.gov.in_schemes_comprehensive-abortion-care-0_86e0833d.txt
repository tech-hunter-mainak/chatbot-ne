Here is a concise summary of the Comprehensive Abortion Care scheme:

**Scheme Overview**

* Provides safe, high-quality abortion services in public sector health facilities
* Focuses on decentralizing services to reach women closer to their homes
* Aimed at reducing unintended pregnancies and abortions

**Training and Service Providers**

* 70 medical officers trained as Master Trainers (2017)
* 317 service providers trained at district level (2017)
* 46% of delivery points from PHC upwards provide MTP services

**Objectives**

* Provide affordable and acceptable abortion services
* Address the needs of young women
* Reduce unintended pregnancies and abortions
* Identify and serve women with their sexual or reproductive health needs
* Ensure sustainability to health systems

**State Initiative**

* 27 districts have District Level Committees
* A "Model CAC Centre" at Assam Medical College & Hospitals, Dibrugarh is being built
