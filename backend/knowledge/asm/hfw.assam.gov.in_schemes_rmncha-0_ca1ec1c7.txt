Here is a concise summary of the relevant information:

**RMNCH+A Scheme**

* Improves maternal and child health and survival in India
* Part of the National Health Mission (NHM) and Millennium Development Goals (MDG)
* Aimed at reducing maternal, newborn, and child mortality
* Includes Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child Plus Adolescent Health (RMNCH+A)

**Programmes under RMNCH+A**

* Reproductive Health: Family Planning, Safe Abortions, Comprehensive Abortion Care
* Maternal Health: Newborn Health, Child Health
* Adolescent Health: Adolescent Friendly Clinics, WIFS

**Services**

* Birth Certificate
* Boat Ambulance and Clinic
* Burn Care Services
* Child Care Leave for Women Employees
* Death Certificate
* Disability Certificate
* Government Blood Banks
* Health Institutions
* HIV Treatment, Counselling and Support
* Jan Aushadhi - Generic Medicines
* Medical Colleges/Institutes
* Mental Health Care
* Mobile Medical Unit
* Organ Transplantation
* PC & PNDT

**Notable Initiatives**

* Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)
* Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)
* Assam Arogya Nidhi (AAN)
