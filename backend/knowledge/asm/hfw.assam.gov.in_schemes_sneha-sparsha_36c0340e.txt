Here is a concise summary of the Sneha Sparsha scheme:

**Scheme Overview:**

* Launched by the Department of Health & Family Welfare, Government of Assam in 2013
* Aimed at providing financial assistance for high-end specialized treatment to children below 12 years of age
* Implemented by the National Health Mission (NHM), Assam

**Eligibility:**

* Families with annual income less than Rs. 2.50 Lakh are eligible
* Priority given to BPL families

**Benefits:**

* Financial assistance for various medical procedures, including:
	+ Bone Marrow Transplant (up to Rs. 10 Lakh)
	+ Cochlear implant (up to Rs. 5.35 Lakh)
	+ Liver Transplant (up to Rs. 16.00 Lakh)
	+ Kidney Transplant (up to Rs. 3.00 Lakh)
	+ Artificial Limb (up to Rs. 2.50 Lakh)
	+ Blood Cancer treatment (up to Rs. 1.00 Lakh)
* Other benefits, including Specialized Eye Surgery and Tumour treatment

**Treatment Options:**

* Some procedures can be treated in Medical College Hospitals or other state hospitals
* Others require referral to suitable Health Institutions outside the state
