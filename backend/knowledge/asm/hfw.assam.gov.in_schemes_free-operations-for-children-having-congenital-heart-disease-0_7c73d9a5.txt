**Scheme Details:**

* Name: Free Operations for Children Having Congenital Heart Disease
* Introduced by: Health & Family Welfare Department, Government of Assam
* Effective date: 01-07-2010
* Eligibility:
	+ Children between 0-14 years old with congenital heart disease
	+ Guardian must be a permanent resident of Assam with income not exceeding Rs. 6 Lakh per annum
* Procedure:
	+ Patient applies to nodal officer with required documents (e.g. medical certificate, address proof)
	+ Nodal officer assesses case and refers patient to referral hospital for surgery
	+ Government bears expenses of surgery, airfare, accommodation for guardian

**Budget Provision:**

* Total budget: Rs. 5920 Lakh
* Fund received and utilized by year (2010-17)
