Here is a concise summary:

**Susrusha Scheme**

* Financial assistance for kidney transplantation
* Launched on 23rd August 2010
* Monthly financial assistance of Rs. 1,00,000 (previously)
* Eligible to residents of Assam with annual income not exceeding Rs. 3,00,000
* Nodal Officer: Dr. Anup Kr. Barman, Director of Medical Education, Assam

**Scheme Details**

* Total fund allocation from 2009-10 to 2016-17: Rs. 7,00,00,000
* Number of beneficiaries:
	+ 44 (2010-11)
	+ 73 (2011-12)
	+ 61 (2012-13)
	+ 47 (2013-14)
	+ 46 (2014-15)
	+ 56 (2015-16)
	+ 59 (2016-17)
