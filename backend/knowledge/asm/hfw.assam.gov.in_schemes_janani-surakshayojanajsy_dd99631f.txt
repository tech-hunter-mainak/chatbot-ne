Here is a concise summary:

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) is a safe motherhood intervention under NRHM, aiming to reduce maternal and neo-natal mortality by promoting institutional delivery among poor pregnant women.

Key features:

- Cash assistance for mothers delivering in government health institutions or accredited private hospitals
- Assistance of Rs. 1400/- for rural area deliveries at public health facilities or accredited private hospitals
- Assistance of Rs. 1000/- for rural area deliveries at public health facilities or accredited private hospitals
- Rs. 500/- provided for home delivery only for BPL category

Objective: Increase institutional delivery among poor families to reduce maternal and neo-natal mortality.
