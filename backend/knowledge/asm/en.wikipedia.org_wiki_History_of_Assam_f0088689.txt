Here's a combined, concise summary of the history of Assam:

**Prehistory**: The region was inhabited by various tribes before Indo-Aryan settlers arrived around 1200 BCE. **Ancient Assam (350-1206)**: Kamarupa kingdom was established in the 4th century CE and reached its traditional extent by the 7th century under Bhaskaravarman. It faced several dynasties, including Mlechchha and Pala, before falling in the 12th century.

**Medieval Assam (1206-1826)**: The Kamata kingdom was established after attacks from Bengalis, followed by the Koch dynasty and Ahom kingdom, which ruled from 1603 to 1826. **Colonial Assam (1826-1947)**: British control was established after the Treaty of Yandaboo in 1826, with Assam becoming a part of Bengal Presidency and later a Chief Commissioner's Province.

**Post-colonial Era**: The Assam Agitation (1979-1985) led by AASU and AAGSP resulted in the signing of the Assam Accord in 1985. Today, Assam is a state in northeastern India with a rich cultural heritage and a diverse population.
