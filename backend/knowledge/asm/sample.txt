Assamese is an Indo-Aryan language spoken mainly in Assam, India. It uses the Assamese script,
a variant of the Eastern Nagari script, also used for Bengali and other regional languages.
Common greetings include "Nomoskar" for hello. Assamese has a rich literary tradition dating
back to the 13th century, including works by Srimanta Sankardev, who founded the Ekasarana
Dharma movement and contributed heavily to Assamese literature, music, and dance (Sattriya).
