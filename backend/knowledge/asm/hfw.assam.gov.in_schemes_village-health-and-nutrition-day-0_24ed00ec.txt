**Village Health and Nutrition Day**

* A program to promote health-related behaviors in communities
* Conducted every Wednesday in identified villages as part of the Sub-centre area's micro plan
* Joint effort of ANM, ASHA, and AWW to benefit the community

**Schemes related to Village Health and Nutrition Day:**

* Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)
* Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)
* Sanjeevani - Village Health Outreach Programme
* Sneha Sparsha
* Susrusha - Financial Assistance For Kidney Transplantation

**Other relevant information:**
