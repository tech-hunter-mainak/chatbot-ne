**Operation Smile**

* A scheme launched by the Government of Assam, Health & Family Welfare department, in collaboration with Operation Smile.
* Objective: Provide free surgery for children having cleft palate and lip.
* Target Group: Patients living with untreated cleft lips or cleft palates.
* Eligibility: All cleft lips or Cleft palates people.
* Total 11,860 nos of children operated under "Operation Smile" since inception till July 14th, 2014.
