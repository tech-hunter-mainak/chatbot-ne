Here is a concise summary of the KAYAKALP scheme:

**Introduction:**
KAYAKALP is a health care initiative to promote cleanliness, hygiene, and infection control practices in public health facilities.

**Objectives:**

* Recognize and reward institutions that adhere to standard protocols of cleanliness and infection control.
* Encourage a culture of ongoing assessment and peer review of performance related to hygiene, cleanliness, and infection control.
* Create sustainable practices related to improved cleanliness in public health facilities linked to positive health outcomes.

**Activities Undertaken in Assam:**

* State Level Award Committee Constitution (2015).
* District Level Award Committee Constitution for all districts.
* Internal, Peer, and External Assessments of Kayakalp in public health institutions.
* Declared awards for district hospitals and sub-centers based on assessment scores.

**Award Winners:**

* Top-performing district hospitals and sub-centers are recognized with awards.
* Winners receive high assessment scores (e.g., 83.7% at Tinsukia Bodirak MPHC).

**Services Provided through KAYAKALP:**

* Various health services, including HIV testing, counseling, and support to PLHIV.
* Maternal and child health services.
* Family planning services.
* Blood banks and medical colleges/institutes.

**KAYAKALP Scheme Benefits:**

* Improved cleanliness and hygiene in public health facilities.
* Enhanced patient care and outcomes.
* Recognition of institutions that adhere to standard protocols.
* Encouragement of sustainable practices related to improved cleanliness.
