Assam Arogya Nidhi (AAN) is a scheme that provides financial assistance to BPL families and those with a monthly income of less than Rs. 10,000 for specialized medical treatment. The scheme covers life-threatening diseases such as heart disease, cancer, kidney disease, orthopedic conditions, and mental illnesses.

Key features:

* Financial assistance up to Rs. 1,50,000 per patient
* Covered diseases: heart disease, cancer, kidney disease, orthopedic conditions, and mental illnesses
* Beneficiaries selected by a Selection Committee notified by the Government of Assam
* Government of India contributes 50% of funds sanctioned by the State Government
