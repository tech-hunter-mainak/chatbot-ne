Here is a concise summary of the Chief Minister's Free Diagnostic Services scheme:

**Overview**

* Aim: Ensure availability of minimum set of diagnostics services at health institutions.
* Objective: Reduce out-of-pocket expenditure on diagnostic tests.

**Services Provided**

* CT Scan:
	+ 28 centers established at 28 district hospitals (24 operational, 4 soon to be operational).
	+ Tele Radiology for reporting and image transmission.
* X-Ray:
	+ All government health institution machines connected through Tele Radiology.
	+ Total of 130 health institutions selected.
* Laboratory Services:
	+ Free services provided at DH/CHC/PHC through Hub and Spoke model.
	+ Sample analysis and report communication within 24 hours for normal cases, 3 hours for emergency cases.

**Benefits**

* All citizens, irrespective of APL/BPL status, eligible for free diagnostics services.

**Implementation**

* Programmed through M/S HLL Lifecare Ltd.
* Installation completed in 27 locations (soon to be operational).
