Here is a concise summary of the webpage:

The website of the Government of Assam's Social Justice & Empowerment Department provides various services and information on social justice, empowerment, and related schemes for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Persons with Disabilities, and other marginalized communities.

Key services include:

* Issuance of certificates (Scheduled Caste, Backward Classes, Non Creamy Layer, Gorkha Community)
* Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarships for Scheduled Castes
* PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for OBCs and Others
* Online registration and grievance redressal

The department is headed by Secretary Shri. Pallav Gopal Jha, IAS.
