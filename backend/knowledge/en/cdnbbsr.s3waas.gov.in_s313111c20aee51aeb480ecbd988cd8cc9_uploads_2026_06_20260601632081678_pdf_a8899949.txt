**Eligibility and Seat Allocation for JEE (Advanced) 2026**

* Candidates with qualifying scores in JEE (Main) 2026 can apply for seats in NIT+ System under GEN or GEN -PwD category.
* Candidates with qualifying scores in JEE (Advanced) 2026 can apply for seats in IITs/IISc under GEN or GEN -PwD category.

**Eligibility Criteria**

* Passing with 75% aggregate marks (65% for SC, ST, and PwD)
* Being within the top 20 percentile of successful candidates
* Class XII mark sheet and certificate are required

**Nationality Status Change**

* If nationality is changed during online reporting, seats will be cancelled and candidates may be considered for subsequent rounds based on updated credentials.

**Grievance Redressal**

* Candidates can contact designated helpdesk numbers or email addresses for queries or grievances related to joint seat allocation process.

**Institutes Admitting Candidates**

1. Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs)
2. Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIITs)

**Annexure 1: Institutes Admitting Candidates**

1-32: IITs
33-44: IIITs
45-79: Other Technical Institutes

**General Marking Rules**

* Marks scored in the final year(s) will be considered for calculating aggregate marks.
* Cut-off marks for top 20 percentile are calculated based on marks scored by successful candidates in their respective boards.

**Document Requirements**

* Class X Mark Sheet/Certificate or Aadhaar Card or Birth Certificate
* Class XII Mark Sheet and Certificate (Qualifying Examination)
* Admit card of JEE (Main) 2026
* Category certificate, if applicable
* Original Certificate for Persons with Disabilities (PwD), if applicable

**Preparatory Courses for SC, ST, and PwD Candidates**

* IITs conduct preparatory courses of one year duration for SC, ST, and PwD candidates.
* Admission is given to candidates in the preparatory course provided seats are vacant.

**Rank Lists**

* Only candidates who appeared for BOTH Paper 1 and Paper 2 of JEE (Advanced) 2026 will be considered for ranking.
