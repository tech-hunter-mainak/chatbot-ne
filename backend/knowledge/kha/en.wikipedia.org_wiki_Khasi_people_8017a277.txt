Here's a combined concise summary:

**Demographics**

* Population: 1,512,831
* Location: India (Meghalaya) and Bangladesh
* Languages: Khasi, Bengali, Assamese
* Region with Austroasiatic influence

**History and Etymology**

* Originated from Ki Hynñiewtrep ("The Seven Huts") according to Khasi mythology
* Closely related to Mon-Khmer people of Southeast Asia
* Traditional Khasi kingdom merged with British India in 1885

**Culture and Traditions**

* Matrilineal society, where property is passed down through women
* Traditional dress: long skirts for men (Jymphong) and women (Jainsem or Dhara)
* Weaving Ryndia silk is a significant cultural symbol
* Monogamous with considerable freedom in choosing partners
* Ritual dances and ceremonies emphasize spiritual practices

**Religion**

* Majority Christian (Catholic & Protestant): 83.14%
* Significant minority: Ka Niam Khasi (15.41%), Hinduism, Buddhism, Islam
* Traditional worship of Kamakhya goddess, later adopted by Hindu and Buddhist traditions

**Economy and Population**

* Known for producing Ryndia silk and other handicrafts
* Estimated population in India and Bangladesh: around 650,000
