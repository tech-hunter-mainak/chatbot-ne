Khasi is an Austroasiatic language spoken primarily by the Khasi people in Meghalaya, India.
Unlike most languages of the region, Khasi is written in the Latin script rather than a
Brahmic script, a legacy of early missionary work. A common greeting is "Khublei", which
means both "hello" and "thank you". Khasi society is traditionally matrilineal, with lineage
and property passing through the mother's line.
