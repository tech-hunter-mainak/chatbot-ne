Here is a concise summary of the Boro people:

**Who are they?**

* The Boro people, also known as the Bodos or Boros, are an indigenous ethnic group living primarily in Assam, India.
* They have their own language, script, and culture.

**Demographics**

* Population: approximately 1.45 million (2011)
* Mainly concentrated in the Bodoland Territorial Region of Assam
* Over two-thirds of the people are bilingual, speaking Assamese as a second language

**History and Identity**

* Boro identity formation began in the colonial period.
* Boros resisted assimilation into Assamese society and developed a distinct identity.
* Kalicharan Brahma established a new monotheistic faith called "Brahma-ism" to assert respectable Bodo identity.

**Culture and Traditions**

* Practiced Bathouism, a form of worship with a supreme god known as Obonglaoree.
* Emphasize the five elements of God: land, water, air, fire, and ether.
* Celebrate the Kherai Puja festival, an important Hindu festival.

**Language and Identity**

* The Boro language is a Tibeto-Burman-speaking language with around 1.5 million speakers in the L1 category.
* The term "Bodo" or "Boro" refers to both the people and their language.
