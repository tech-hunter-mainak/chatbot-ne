Construction of community ponds scheme:

* Department: Fisheries Department
* Description: Creation of community ponds to increase fish production in the state.
* Beneficiaries:
	+ Farmers
	+ Individual
	+ Community
* Scheme benefits:
	+ Subsidy
* Eligibility criteria:
	+ Age: 18-60 years
* Funding:
	+ 54% Central share
	+ 6% State share
	+ 40% Beneficiary share
* Introduced: January 1, 2018
