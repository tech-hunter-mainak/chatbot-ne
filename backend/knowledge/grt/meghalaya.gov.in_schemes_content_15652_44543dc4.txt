Anganwadi Services (ICDS)

* Description: Non-formal pre-school education, supplementary nutrition programme, immunization, health check-up, nutrition and health education, and referral service.
* Scheme Beneficiaries:
	+ Children 6 months to 6 years old
	+ Pregnant, nursing mothers
	+ Adolescent girls 11-19 years
* Scheme Benefits: Other (unspecified)
* Funding: 10:90 ratio
* Introduced on: April 1, 2007
