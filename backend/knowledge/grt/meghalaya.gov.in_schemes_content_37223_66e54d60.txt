**Community Fishery Development Project**

* Department: Fisheries Department
* Scheme Title: Community Fishery Development Project
* Description: Financial assistance for new pond/tank renovation and input subsidy (up to 50%)
* Beneficiaries: Farmers with minimum water area of 0.50 ha or 5000 sq.m
* Funding: 100%
* Introduced: April 1, 2007
