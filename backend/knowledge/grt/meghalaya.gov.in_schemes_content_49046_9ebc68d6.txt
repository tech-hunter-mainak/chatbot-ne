**Chief Minister's Regional Transport Scheme 2025**

The CM - Regional Transport Scheme is a flagship initiative to establish a reliable bus network in Meghalaya, linking Block HQs with District HQs.

* Objective: Ensure connectivity to all regions and provide fair access to essential services and economic participation.
* Scheme emphasis: Entrepreneurship-driven public transport service delivery.
* Financial support: Up to 50% of the total cost of the vehicle, including loan interest, for eligible entrepreneurs operating bus services on designated routes.
* Beneficiaries: Anyone.
* Age requirement: 18-58 years.
* Associated scheme: Financial Assistance.
* Sponsors: State government.

The Scheme aims to create a reliable and efficient transportation network that connects remote regions with key economic and administrative centers.
