Chief Minister's Youth Development Scheme (CMYDS) Guidelines:

**Objectives:**

1. Provide creative outlets for youth to positively channel their energies.
2. Strengthen commitment of youth to nation building and national unity.
3. Develop cooperative attitudes between administration and youth.
4. Enhance skills and productivity of youth.
5. Increase awareness among youth for social and economic development.

**Scheme Beneficiaries:**

1. Physically challenged (Differently abled)
2. Unemployed
3. Students
4. Citizens
5. Urban and rural youth

**Scheme Benefits:**

1. Financial aid to registered entities through Deputy Commissioners' office.

**Eligibility Age Range:** 14-29 years old
