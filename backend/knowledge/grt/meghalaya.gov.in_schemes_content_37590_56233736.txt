Award for Meritorious Students of Meghalaya in the HSSLC Examination (Science Stream)

* Description:
	+ List of students furnished by MBOSE after HSSLC result declaration
	+ Screening of documents by DERT
	+ DERT transfers one-time scholarship directly to student's bank account through NEFT/RTGS
* Beneficiaries: Students from ST community
* Scheme Benefits: Scholarship (100% funding)
* How to Avail: Offline from DERT
* Sponsors: State government
