**Primary School Scholarship and Upper Primary Scholarship**

* Conducted by: Directorate of Educational Research and Training (DERT)
* Objective: Identify students with academic talent, provide platform for competitive examinations, and financial assistance.
* Eligibility:
	+ Students studying in class V and VIII
	+ All schools in the state
* Benefits: Scholarship
* Application process:
	1. Students apply through schools to Sub-Divisional School Education Officer (SDSEO)
	2. SDSEOs send student list to DERT
	3. DERT conducts exam
	4. Selected candidates receive scholarship from Directorate of State Examination and Literacy (DSEL)
* Sponsors: State
* Funding: 100% by the state
