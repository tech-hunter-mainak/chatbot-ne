West Garo Hills district is one of the largest districts in Meghalaya, located in the western part of the state. The district was formed in October 1976 and further divided into two administrative districts in June 1992. Tura is the district headquarters and second-largest town in Meghalaya.

The district is predominantly inhabited by the Garo tribe with a matrilineal society, along with other indigenous groups such as Hajongs, Rabhas, and Bengalis. The terrain is mostly hilly with plains on its borders, featuring three important mountain ranges: Tura Range, Arbella Range, and Ranggira Range.

The district's topography includes rivers such as Kalu, Didak, Bogai, Jinjiram, and Dareng, which flow towards the Bangladesh plains in the south and the Brahmaputra valley in the north. The highest peak is Nokrek (1412m) located in the Tura range.
