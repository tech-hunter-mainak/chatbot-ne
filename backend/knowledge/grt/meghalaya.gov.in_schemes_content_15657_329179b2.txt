**Anganwadi Building Construction Scheme**

* Department: Social Welfare Department
* Title: Construction of Anganwadi buildings for conducting ICDS Programmes
* Beneficiaries: Children below 6 years, pregnant and nursing mothers
* Benefits: Provision of building facilities
* Eligibility:
	+ Age: None specified
	+ Application to CDPO office of ICDS project
* Funding: 90:10 ratio
* Introduced on: April 1, 2007
