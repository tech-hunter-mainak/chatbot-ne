Border Areas Scholarship Scheme:

* Introduced in 1973
* Aimed at providing financial assistance to students from international border areas due to economic hardships
* Benefits: Scholarship, stipend
* Sponsors: State government
* Eligibility:
	+ Age: None (assuming it refers to any age)
	+ No specific income limit
* Application process: Offline applications through Head of Educational Institutions to Directorate of Higher & Technical Education, Shillong
