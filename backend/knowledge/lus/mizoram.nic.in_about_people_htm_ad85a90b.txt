The people of Mizoram are a close-knit society with no class distinction or discrimination based on sex. They have a unique code of ethics called "Tlawmngaihna" which means being hospitable, kind, unselfish, and helpful to others. The majority of the population (95%) is Christian.

The social fabric of Mizoram has undergone changes over the years, with a focus on education, family, and community. The village and clan are the units of Mizo society, and traditional festivals such as Mim Kut, Pawl Kut, and Chapchar Kut celebrate agriculture operations and harvest seasons.

Mizos have a rich cultural heritage, including traditional clothing such as puanchei, kawrchei, ngotekherh, hmar am, and cyhna hno. Music is an important part of Mizo culture, with a focus on traditional instruments and western music.
