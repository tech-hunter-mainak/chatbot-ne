Customs of the Mizo People:

* Bride Price: The principal price is Manpui (Rs 80/- per unit), with subsidiary prices like sumhmahruai (Rs 20/-) and sumfang (Rs 8/-.)
* Payments to relatives include:
	+ Naupuakpuan to elder sisters or sisters of the bride
	+ Ni-ar to the paternal aunt
	+ Thianman to the bride's maid
	+ Pusum to the nearest relation on the side of the bride's mother
* Wedding customs:
	+ Mizo marriage is preceded by courtship and engagement
	+ Marriages are solemnized in Church with Western-style wedding dresses
	+ A traditional rug called Puandum is brought to her husband as a burial cloth
* Types of marriages:
	+ Makpa chhungkhung: no bride price, the son-in-law lives with his wife's parents
	+ Luhkhung: performed without social ceremony, often in the Vestry
	+ Tlandun: couple runs away from home to get married
* Inheritance:
	+ Property is inherited by men, usually going to the youngest son
	+ Daughters can inherit property if there are no male heirs on the male side
