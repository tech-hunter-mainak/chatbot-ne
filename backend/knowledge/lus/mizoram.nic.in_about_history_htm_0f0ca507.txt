Here is a concise summary:

**Mizoram's History**

* The Mizos migrated from China around the 5th century and settled in Burma.
* In 1959, a famine hit Mizoram due to rat infestations after bamboo flowering.
* The Mizo National Front (MNF) was formed in 1961 to achieve independence, leading to large-scale disturbances in 1966 and subsequent outlawing in 1967.

**Statehood**

* In 1972, the Union Territory of Mizoram was formed, with Laldenga meeting Prime Minister Indira Gandhi to discuss statehood.
* A peace agreement was signed between the MNF and the Union Government in 1986, leading to the formalization of the state on February 20, 1987.

**Places of Interest**

* Historic sites include:
	+ Blue Mountain (Phawngpui), highest peak with breathtaking views
	+ Pukzing Cave, largest cave with human skeletons
	+ Lamsial Puk, silent testimony to a battle between two villages
	+ Sibuta Lung, memorial stone erected after a tribal chief went mad for revenge
	+ Darlalpuii, gravestone with the story of a girl crushed alive under a weighty stone
	+ Mangkhai Lung, memorial stone for a well-known Ralte chief

**Other Notable Sites**

* Thangliana Lung, memorial stone dedicated to Captain T.H.Lewin, one of the first Englishmen to come to Mizoram
* Suangpuilawn Inscriptions, ancient words inscribed on a stone slab
