Here is a concise summary:

Mizoram has a diverse physiography with:

* 21 major hills and ranges, including Phawngpui (Blue Mountain) at 2,065m
* Variegated topography, including rugged hills and scattered plains
* Important rivers:
	+ Tlawng (Dhaleswari or Katakhal)
	+ Tut (Gutur)
	+ Tuirial (Sonai)
	+ Tuivawl
	+ Koldoyne (Chhimtuipui) from Myanmar
* Important lakes:
	+ Palak Lake in Chhimtuipui District, covering 30 Ha
	+ Tamdil Lake, a natural lake with a unique legend behind its name
