Mizo is a Tibeto-Burman language spoken by the Mizo people, mainly in Mizoram, India. It
is written in the Latin script and has a tonal system. A common greeting is "Chibai" or
"Kan lawm e" for "thank you". Mizo society historically follows "Tlawmngaihna", a code of
conduct emphasizing selflessness, hospitality, and helping others without expecting return.
